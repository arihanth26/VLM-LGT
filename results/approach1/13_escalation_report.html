Approach 1, report 13: larger models as fixes, and as candidates for the ranker

The 2B model's own corrections (reports 10–12) reach 86.38%. This report adds fixes from outside the 2B model: the answers of Qwen3-VL-8B and 4B, and the 2B model on enlarged images. They are used two ways: on their own (route the least-confident questions to the larger model) and as extra candidates for the ranker. Qwen3-VL-2B stays the detector and the encoder of every candidate. Full test splits, 9,517 questions; every choice made on validation (6,420 questions) and test read once; rankers trained on 12,000 training questions.

  • The result is stable. Two further seed sets move the best ranker by less than 0.1 point; trained without one dataset it keeps most of its gain there; and it reaches 89.20% with a quarter of the training questions (Section 11).

  • Measured time changes the cost picture. On one H100 an 8B short answer takes 0.16 s against 0.12 s for the 2B one, while a step-by-step answer takes 1.6–2.0 s. Counted in seconds, the ranker over the greedy and 8B answers reaches 88.34% at 0.26 s per question (Section 14).

Two of the pass-counting conventions do not match measured time. An 8B short answer counts as 4 passes but takes 1.3 times a 2B answer: at batch size 1 the GPU is far from saturated, so time grows much more slowly than parameter count. A step-by-step answer counts as 1 call but takes 14 times a short answer, because it generates up to 256–384 tokens. In seconds, an 8B answer costs about as much as one zoomed-crop answer and is the most effective single fix, while the reasoning candidates are the most expensive (Section 4 gives the policies chosen under seconds caps).

16. Limits

  • Two helpers from other families were tested (InternVL3-8B, Idefics3-8B); the detector and the ranker are still Qwen3-VL-2B.
  • Pass counts are a convention; Section 14 shows they misstate the relative cost of 8B answers and reasoning.
  • Latency is measured at batch size 1 on one GPU type; batched serving would change the ratios.
  • The ranker is trained with correctness labels on four datasets; Sections 11 and 12 test how far it carries.
